\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\para{Benchmark saturation and dynamics.}
Benchmarks in NLP and machine learning are saturated faster than they are created. \citet{kiela2021dynabench} document how quickly models reach human-level scores on static benchmarks and propose dynamic, adversarial data collection as a remedy. \citet{ott2022mapping} map the life cycle of thousands of benchmarks and show that many saturate within a few years or stop being used. \citet{bowman2021will} argue that benchmarks must keep being valid and discriminative near the top of the range to remain useful. These works describe {\em that} benchmarks saturate. We ask {\em which design properties} decide the accuracy at which a benchmark stops separating contemporaneous models.

\para{Item response theory for model evaluation.}
IRT models the probability that a respondent solves an item as a function of ability and item difficulty~\citep{rasch1960probabilistic,lord1980applications}. \citet{lalor2016building} and \citet{vania2021comparing} brought IRT to NLP test sets, and \citet{rodriguez2021evaluation} showed that items are not equally informative for leaderboard ranking. More recently, tinyBenchmarks~\citep{polo2024tinybenchmarks} and metabench~\citep{kipnis2024metabench} use IRT to compress LLM benchmarks to a small, informative core, and adaptive testing has been proposed for LLMs~\citep{zhuang2023efficiently}. Building on this line, we use IRT not to compress benchmarks but to ask how the difficulty distribution controls the saturation point, and we check every IRT-based claim on real LLM responses.

\para{Variance and noise in LLM evaluation.}
\citet{madaan2024quantifying} quantify seed variance across training runs and show that it can rival the gaps between models on several benchmarks. \citet{miller2024adding} argues for reporting standard errors based on item sampling. Multiple-choice evaluations are also sensitive to option order~\citep{zheng2024large,pezeshkpour2024large} and to prompt formatting~\citep{sclar2024quantifying}. Holistic evaluation suites report many such factors side by side~\citep{liang2023holistic}. Unlike these works, we measure evaluation-seed noise as a function of how saturated the benchmark is, and we compare it directly with the capability spread of the models being ranked, so that we can say when seed noise dominates and when it does not.
